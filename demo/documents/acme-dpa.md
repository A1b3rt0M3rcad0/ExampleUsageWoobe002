## 3. Processing
Acme Cloud acts as processor for customer personal data and processes data only on documented customer instructions.

## 7. Security incidents
Acme Cloud will notify the customer without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after confirming a security incident affecting customer personal data. Acme will reasonably cooperate with the customer's investigation.

## 12. Subprocessors
Acme maintains a public subprocessor register. New subprocessors may be appointed after 15 days prior notice. Customers may object on reasonable data-protection grounds during the notice period.

## 15. International transfers
Where customer personal data is transferred from the EEA to a jurisdiction without an adequacy decision, the parties will use the applicable Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 18. Termination
After termination and upon customer request, Acme will make customer data available for export for 15 days and will delete remaining customer data within 45 days after that export period, except where retention is required by law.
