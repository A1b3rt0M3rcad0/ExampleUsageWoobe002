# Atlas Compliance Assessment Methodology

Evaluate CMP-01 through CMP-06 when applicable.

## Requirement risk points

- PASS: 0
- NOT_APPLICABLE: excluded
- NOT_EVIDENCED: 60
- FAIL/LOW: 25
- FAIL/MEDIUM: 45
- FAIL/HIGH: 80
- FAIL/CRITICAL: 100

Domain risk score = mean of applicable requirement points, rounded to nearest integer.

The output states conformance to Atlas demo requirements only.
Never write "GDPR compliant", "SOC 2 compliant", "certified", or equivalent unless the exact vendor evidence justifies the narrower factual statement.
