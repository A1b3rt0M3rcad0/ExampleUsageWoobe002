# Atlas Demo Compliance Requirements

These requirements are fictitious Atlas internal demo policy and do not constitute certification or legal advice.

## CMP-01 — PII processing basis

When PII is handled, evidence must identify data-processing terms/DPA or equivalent processing obligations.
Missing evidence is NOT_EVIDENCED/HIGH.

## CMP-02 — Data location/transfers

For US/EU assessments involving PII, evidence should identify hosting/data-location information and a mechanism or disclosure addressing cross-border transfers where relevant.
Missing evidence is NOT_EVIDENCED/MEDIUM.

## CMP-03 — Subprocessor transparency

For PII vendors, evidence should provide a subprocessor list/register or a defined mechanism to obtain it.
No transparency mechanism is FAIL/MEDIUM.

## CMP-04 — Retention and deletion

Evidence should define customer-data retention/deletion behavior, including post-termination handling.
No evidence is NOT_EVIDENCED/HIGH.

## CMP-05 — Assurance evidence

HIGH/CRITICAL vendors should provide current audit/certification/independent assurance evidence appropriate to the service.
Do not treat a logo or unsupported statement as current assurance.
Missing evidence is NOT_EVIDENCED/MEDIUM.

## CMP-06 — Incident/regulatory cooperation

For PII vendors, evidence should support reasonable cooperation with customer regulatory/security incident obligations.
Explicit exclusion of cooperation is FAIL/HIGH.
