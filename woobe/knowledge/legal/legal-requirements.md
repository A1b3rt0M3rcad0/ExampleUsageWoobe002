# Atlas Demo Legal Requirements

These requirements are fictitious Atlas internal demo policy and are not legal advice.

## LEG-01 — Subprocessors

For PII-processing vendors, the contract/DPA should require:
- notice before a material new subprocessor; and
- a reasonable customer objection mechanism.

Thirty days notice satisfies the Atlas demo target.
No notice mechanism is FAIL/HIGH.

## LEG-02 — Security incident cooperation

The contract should require cooperation with investigation and reasonable information needed for customer incident response.
Explicit refusal/absence of cooperation language is FAIL/MEDIUM.

## LEG-03 — Liability

The general liability cap should be at least 12 months of fees under this demo policy.
Data confidentiality breach, gross negligence or willful misconduct must not be silently treated as fully excluded from liability unless evidence says so.
An explicit cap below 12 months for all claims is FAIL/HIGH.

## LEG-04 — Termination data handling

The agreement should provide a mechanism for return/export and deletion of customer data after termination.
Atlas target: deletion within 30 days after the agreed retention/return window.
No documented deletion mechanism is FAIL/HIGH.

## LEG-05 — Service level

For HIGH/CRITICAL operational vendors, target service availability is at least 99.9% with a defined service-credit/remedy mechanism.
Explicit SLA below 99.9% is FAIL/MEDIUM.

## LEG-06 — Data processing terms

When PII is handled, assessment evidence should contain data-processing terms that identify processing roles/instructions and address security, subprocessors and cross-border transfers where relevant.
Missing DPA/data-processing terms is NOT_EVIDENCED/HIGH.
