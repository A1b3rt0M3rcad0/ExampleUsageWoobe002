# Atlas Legal Assessment Methodology

Evaluate LEG-01 through LEG-06 when applicable.

## Requirement risk points

- PASS: 0
- NOT_APPLICABLE: excluded
- NOT_EVIDENCED: 60
- FAIL/LOW: 25
- FAIL/MEDIUM: 45
- FAIL/HIGH: 80
- FAIL/CRITICAL: 100

Domain risk score = mean of applicable requirement points, rounded to nearest integer.

Contract wording can be conditional and distributed across sections. When a search result is ambiguous, call atlas_get_evidence before deciding.

A clause that merely permits a practice is not equivalent to a clause requiring that practice.
Do not infer an obligation from a heading alone.
